# Contributing Guide

Wizard's Reborn is the personal Minecraft mod of [MaxBogomol (Pink Joke)](https://github.com/MaxBogomol).

When proposing a modification, please comply with License.  
If you want to suggest new mechanics or content, just write about it and don't do it yourself, I am developing this mod myself.  
Bug fixes are allowed.  
Translations into other languages are welcomed.
